About

Engineering that balances performance, clarity and maintainability.

I'm a senior full stack developer specializing in the PHP/Laravel ecosystem. Over the past 7+ years I've worked across agencies, freelance engagements and international remote teams, delivering web applications that have to stay fast and reliable in production.

My work sits between backend architecture and interface craft: designing relational data models, building secure RESTful APIs, and wiring reactive frontends with Livewire and Vue.js. I profile before optimizing — Redis caching, queue-driven workloads, and SQL that stays predictable as data grows.

I've collaborated with clients and cross-functional teams across Saudi Arabia and Tunisia, and I'm comfortable owning a feature from technical analysis through testing, deployment and iteration.

How I work
